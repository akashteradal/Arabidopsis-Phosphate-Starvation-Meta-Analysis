# Gene Ontology Enrichment Analysis

This directory contains the Gene Ontology (GO) Biological Process enrichment analysis performed on the conserved gene sets identified from the comparative meta-analysis.

## Analysis

The conserved genes identified from the short-term and long-term meta-analyses were separated according to their direction of expression and analyzed independently using **ShinyGO**.

The analysis focused on **Gene Ontology Biological Process (GO-BP)** enrichment.

## Short-term Enrichment

The short-term analysis represented the conserved transcriptional response to phosphate starvation at 1–2 hours.

The conserved short-term genes were separated into:

- **Upregulated genes**
- **Downregulated genes**

GO Biological Process enrichment was performed separately for the short-term upregulated and downregulated gene sets.

## Long-term Enrichment

The long-term analysis represented the conserved transcriptional response to phosphate starvation at 8–21 days.

The conserved long-term genes were separated into:

- **Upregulated genes**
- **Downregulated genes**

GO Biological Process enrichment was performed separately for the long-term upregulated and downregulated gene sets.


## Significance Threshold

Significantly enriched biological processes were identified using:

**FDR < 0.05**

The resulting GO Biological Process enrichment results were used to interpret the biological functions associated with conserved acute and chronic phosphate starvation responses.
