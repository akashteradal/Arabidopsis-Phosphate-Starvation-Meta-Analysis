# Meta-analysis

This directory contains the results of the comparative meta-analysis performed across the independent RNA-seq datasets.

Differentially expressed gene lists from the individual datasets were compared using **InteractiVenn** to identify conserved transcriptional responses to phosphate starvation.

## Short-term Meta-analysis

The short-term analysis represents phosphate starvation responses at:

- 1 hour
- 2 hours

The short-term DEGs were separated into:

- **Upregulated genes**
- **Downregulated genes**

The upregulated and downregulated gene lists from the short-term datasets were intersected separately to identify conserved genes showing consistent direction of expression across the acute phosphate-starvation response.

## Long-term Meta-analysis

The long-term analysis represents phosphate starvation responses at:

- 8 days
- 21 days

The long-term DEGs were also separated into:

- **Upregulated genes**
- **Downregulated genes**

The upregulated and downregulated gene lists from the long-term datasets were intersected separately to identify conserved genes showing consistent direction of expression during chronic phosphate starvation.

## Comparative Analysis

The conserved upregulated and downregulated gene sets obtained from the short-term and long-term intersections were used for downstream biological interpretation.

This approach allowed the study to distinguish:

- Conserved upregulation during acute phosphate starvation
- Conserved downregulation during acute phosphate starvation
- Conserved upregulation during chronic phosphate starvation
- Conserved downregulation during chronic phosphate starvation

The resulting conserved gene sets were subsequently used for Gene Ontology Biological Process enrichment analysis.
